Definitions

How the numbers are built.

Where do the complaint numbers come from?
From the consolidated complaints file, tab CPM - NOMC-E2E-REGIONS, counted by month. FOPs team is the Routing Team (C1–C6) and region is the Sub Region.
What counts as an OFC cut?
Rows flagged OFC in the break logs, counted by the month of their date. A site is "on a cut section" when its id appears in the sections text.
How are availability and load shedding measured?
As the plain average over sites for each month. Restoration time is the median duration of a fiber break.
How do I read the correlation (r)?
r is the Pearson correlation of monthly values. Closer to +1 or −1 means the two measures move together more tightly; wording on the dashboards changes at 0.3 and 0.5.
Which figures compare with 2025?
The OFC break logs and the site availability and load shedding file cover 2025 and 2026, so those figures are compared with the same months of 2025. The complaints data covers 2026 only.
Why are some sites or cities left out of comparisons?
Site buckets need at least 30 sites per cell and 10 per attribute group. "DG Khan" is merged with "Dera Ghazi Khan" in the city movers table only.
